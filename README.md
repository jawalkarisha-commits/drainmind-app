# 🌧️ DrainMind — Urban Waterlogging Risk Predictor

**Predict potential drainage blockages. Prioritize inspections. Help communities prepare for waterlogging.**

## 📌 Overview

DrainMind is a proposed urban drainage monitoring and risk-prioritization system designed to help identify drains that may require urgent inspection.

It aims to combine rainfall information, citizen-submitted drain reports, blockage observations, and location data to highlight potentially high-risk areas on an interactive map.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Blocked stormwater drains, unmanaged waste, and heavy rainfall can contribute to urban waterlogging. Municipal teams may struggle to identify which drainage locations need attention first.

DrainMind aims to make drain reporting and inspection prioritization more organized and data-driven.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

DrainMind will provide a dashboard for collecting drain reports, assessing potential risk, visualizing reported locations, and tracking maintenance actions.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Citizen Drain Reporting:** Submit a drain location, blockage level, description, and optional photograph.
- **Risk Prioritization:** Calculate a preliminary risk score using available rainfall and drainage-report data.
- **Interactive Risk Map:** Display reported drains and their estimated risk levels.
- **Maintenance Dashboard:** Track reports as pending, assigned, or cleared.
- **Photo-Assisted Assessment:** Explore AI-assisted identification of potential drain blockages, subject to testing and human verification.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js, Vite, JavaScript, CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Maps:** Leaflet and OpenStreetMap
- **AI:** To be evaluated for photo-assisted blockage assessment

## 🗺️ Expected Workflow

1. A citizen or field worker submits a drain report.
2. The system records its location and observed condition.
3. Available data is used to calculate a preliminary risk score.
4. The map highlights locations that may need priority inspection.
5. Municipal staff update the maintenance status.

## ⚠️ Important Note

DrainMind is a proposed prototype. Risk scores are intended for preliminary prioritization, not guaranteed flood predictions. Real-world accuracy will depend on data quality, testing, and validation.

## 🚀 Project Status

Planning and initial development.

## 🎯 Goal

To support more efficient drainage inspections and help communities identify potentially vulnerable drainage locations.
